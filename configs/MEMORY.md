   * A non-refundable application/registration processing fee of ₦10,000 applies to all new students.
   * Default on instalment payment beyond the 7-day grace period automatically suspends access to the live virtual classroom and Git repository until regularized.

2. **Refund Policy (Absolute Standard):**
   * Before Day 1 of classes: 80% tuition refundable upon written request (20% retained + ₦10,000 registration fee non-refundable).
   * After Day 1 of classes: **0% refund**. No exceptions.

3. **Deferral Policy:**
   * A student may request to defer admission to the subsequent cohort once.
   * Request must be logged at least 7 days prior to cohort commencement.
   * Deferral approval is classified as a **Tier 3 Administrative Action** and must be approved by the Academic Director via Telegram Approval Gate.

4. **Graduation Requirements:**
   * Minimum 80% attendance in lectures and practical labs.
   * 100% completion of all module assessments.
   * Successful completion and presentation of the Capstone Project.
